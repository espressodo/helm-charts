LOCAL INGRESS TESTING (e.g., Minikube/Docker Desktop):
If your Ingress is not reachable directly via the host, use a Port-Forward:

1) Forward the Ingress Controller:
   kubectl -n ingress-nginx port-forward svc/ingress-nginx-controller 9443:443

2) Access via Browser:
   https://localhost:9443 (Accept the self-signed certificate if prompted)

3) Verify Backend via Ingress:
   curl -vk -H "Host: {{ .Values.ingress.host }}" https://localhost:9443/api/v1/oidc-conf
